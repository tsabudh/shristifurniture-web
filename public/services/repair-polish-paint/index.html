Services

Repair, Polish & Painting

A loose joint, a scratched top or a faded finish does not mean the piece has to go. We repair, strip, polish or paint it, often for far less than a new one.

Request a quote

How it works

  1. 01

    Inspect

    We look at the frame, joints and surface and tell you honestly what can be saved.

  2. 02

    Strip & repair

    Old finish is removed by hand and sanding. Broken joints are re-glued and missing parts replaced.

  3. 03

    Colour & finish

    Choose a natural stain, a clear polish or paint. We match an existing colour on request.

  4. 04

    Polish & return

    Several coats are applied and cured, then the piece is checked and returned to you.

Finishes

  • Wood stain and clear polish
  • Melamine and PU lacquer, from matt to gloss
  • Enamel and wood paint in any colour
  • Hand-sanding for a smooth base
  • Touch-up for scratches and water marks
