About us

Shristi Furniture makes and sells furniture for homes, offices, hotels and shops. We use seasoned wood and sturdy hardware, and every piece is checked before it leaves the workshop.

You can buy ready-made pieces from the showroom or have anything made to your size, wood and finish. We also repair, polish and re-upholster furniture you already own.
